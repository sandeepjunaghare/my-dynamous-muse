# Outreach messaging constraints

Load this when generating, templating or reviewing any outreach copy — email, call opener, door script.

These are product constraints carried forward from the playbook, not style preferences, and anything the
system generates must respect them:

- **Never lead with AI.** Three documented rejections cite it: Eagle One (09-18), "already have AI and happy
  with it" (09-22), Crisp-LaDew (09-22) — and the CRM records the opener "local AI expert for Fire and Alarms
  company" being used twice against this very rule (E16). Lead with the friction the owner already feels.
- **Do not pitch during discovery.** "The moment you pitch, you stop learning and you lose the referral."
- **Capture their exact words**, not a paraphrase — their phrasing becomes the outreach copy.
- **Always get a number** — hours, dollars, headcount or percentage. "Without a number you have an anecdote,
  not an opportunity."
- **Price is spoken, not written:** "nine hundred ninety-nine dollars", never "nine ninety-nine".

Two things gate the email leg regardless of copy: the opener has not been rewritten yet (nobody owns it), and
nothing sends from any domain until Spike 4 closes. Assume outreach copy is drafted for a human to send.
